How to use a voice changer on Discord

To send your Micle voice into Discord, you use a "virtual microphone". Micle plays your voice into the virtual mic, and Discord uses that virtual mic as its microphone. You only set it up once.

What you need

  • Micle (free)
  • VB-CABLE (a free virtual microphone by VB-Audio Software; installable from inside Micle)
  • Discord (desktop app)

How to do it

  1. Install VB-CABLE

    In Micle, go to Settings → Devices → VB-CABLE and press "Download and install". It downloads from the official source; press "Install Driver" in the installer, then restart your PC.

  2. Check Micle's output

    After restarting, make sure Settings → Devices → Output is CABLE Input (it is selected automatically when found).

  3. Change Discord's input device

    In Discord, go to User Settings → Voice & Video → Input Device and choose "CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)".

    On the same screen, turn off Discord's noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain control, and turn off automatic input sensitivity and set it low. Otherwise sound effects and music may get cut and your voice may sound muffled. Micle mixes in your real microphone, so you don't need to pick your mic again in Discord.

  4. Choose a voice

    On Micle's Voice Changer page, pick a preset (girl, man, child, robot, phone, radio and more — 24 in total) and turn the voice changer ON. Fine-tune pitch, formant and effects.

  5. Check how others hear you

    Under Other → Test, "How others hear you" records 5 seconds and plays it back. Try it once before a call.

Make it handier

  • Switch with a key: set a voice key to tap ON/OFF, or hold it and scroll to change pitch.
  • Your own voices: save a tuned voice with "Save current voice" and recall it with one key.
  • With noise removal: AI noise removal and auto volume make changed voices easier to hear.
Please don't use a changed voice to impersonate others or to harass anyone.

FAQ

People can't hear me

Check that Discord's input device is "CABLE Output" and that "On air" is ON in Micle. The Test page checks everything at once.

I want to hear my changed voice

Raise "Hear your own voice" on the Voice Changer page to monitor the processed voice in your headphones.

Does it work outside Discord?

Yes. Any app that lets you choose a microphone (game voice chat, Zoom, Teams, OBS…) works if you select "CABLE Output".
