Handy tricks while gaming

Things I wished I could do while gaming with friends are built into Micle. Each one works with a single key.

How to do it

  1. Open the quick menu

    Hold (or tap several times) your chosen key to open a small panel with volume, device battery, toggles and notes. It can be hidden from streams. Set it up under Other → Quick menu.

  2. Move a window behind your game to another monitor

    The shortcut "Move the window behind to the next monitor" moves a browser or other window from behind your fullscreen game without closing it.

  3. Move the mouse to the next monitor

    The shortcut "Move the mouse to the next monitor" jumps across wide setups instantly.

  4. Crosshair in the center

    The shortcut "Show ⇔ hide a crosshair in the center" draws one on top of the screen.

    Crosshairs and macros may be against the rules of some games. Check the game's rules before using them.

  5. Record the moment as a GIF

    The shortcut "Record part of the screen as a GIF" saves the area you select as a GIF, ready to share.

  6. Notes & checklists

    Write strategy notes or item checklists in Micle's notes and pin them to see them from the quick menu.

  7. Game and app volume

    The shortcuts "Front app volume up / down" change volume without leaving the game.

  8. Switch scenes at once

    Save your current settings as scenes like "Gaming" or "Chatting" and switch with one key — or automatically when a chosen app comes to the front.

FAQ

Where do I create shortcuts?

Under Other → Shortcuts: pick a type and assign a key. There are 99 types.
