Remove noise from your calls (AI noise removal)

During gaming calls, your mic easily picks up keyboard and mouse clicks, PC fans and household noise. Micle's AI noise removal keeps only human voices and removes the rest. Everything is processed on your PC.

What you need

  • Micle (free)
  • Discord or another app set up to use Micle's voice (Discord setup)

How to do it

  1. Turn on noise removal

    On Micle's Voice Changer page, turn on "Keep only human voice" under Noise removal. You can also toggle it from "Noise removal" in the top bar.

  2. Adjust the strength

    Use "Strength" under Advanced. Too strong can muffle your voice, so stop where the noise is no longer bothersome.

  3. Silence small sounds between words

    Raise "Mute non-voice" a little to fully remove sounds that don't look like a voice. Great for keyboard clicks.

  4. Even out your volume

    Turn on "Even out your voice level" under Auto volume to boost quiet speech and tame loud speech.

  5. Check how others hear you

    Use "How others hear you" on the Test page to record and play back.

You can also clean up other people's voices

With Pro, Micle can remove noise from the other people's voices for your headphones. Nothing changes on their side.

FAQ

Can I use it with Discord's noise suppression?

We recommend turning Discord's noise suppression off. Stacking both can muffle your voice and even cut sound effects and music. Let Micle handle the noise.

Will it slow down my games?

Noise removal is lightweight. "Processing load (DSP)" in the top bar shows how much it uses.
