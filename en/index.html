Everything I wanted

Micle is a Windows app that supports your voice calls, games and streams from the side. Voice changer, noise removal, sound effects, avatars and captions over your game, one-key shortcuts, Minecraft port forwarding and notes — all in one. The core features are free forever, with no ads.

A little something to share

While talking with friends during games, I kept wanting things: "I want a sound effect right now", "I want to move a window behind my game without closing it", "I want to open my Minecraft server quickly".

I built them one by one into a single app, and it became surprisingly handy. So I am sharing it.

Features

All in one app. Turn off what you don't use and it disappears from the screen and stops running, so it stays light and simple.

Voice changer

Pitch, formant and effects. 24 presets such as girl, robot, phone and cave, sent straight into Discord and other calls.

AI noise removal & auto volume

Removes keyboard, fan and room noise so only your voice gets through, and evens out quiet and loud speech.

Sound effects, BGM, text-to-speech

Sound effects on a single key. Play your own music as BGM, automatically lowered while you talk.

Overlays (avatar, captions, now playing)

Show an avatar, clock, timer, now-playing song or captions on top of your game — also visible in streams.

Quick menu

A small panel you can open mid-game by holding or tapping a key: volume, device battery, toggles and notes.

Shortcuts & macros

99 one-key actions: open apps, move windows, volume, brightness, text conversion and more. Plus record-and-replay macros.

Move windows to another monitor

Move a window that is behind your fullscreen game to the next monitor without closing the game.

Port forwarding & network

Open ports for game servers like Minecraft with one button, no router settings page needed. Firewall and connection checks too.

Notes & checklists

Write and find notes instantly. Checklists, tags, pins and due-date reminders.

Play instruments

Turn your keyboard into a piano, with drums, accompaniment and a looper for jamming in calls.

Morse code

Send typed text as Morse tones and decode incoming Morse back to text. Practice mode included.

Minutes (transcription)

Transcribe calls entirely on your PC. Your voice never leaves your computer.

Party tools & whiteboard

Dice, roulette, lottery, polls, buzzer, bingo, and pens to draw on the screen.

Window translation PRO

Reads text in games and apps and overlays a live translation, fully on your PC.

AI images & avatars PRO

Describe a picture to create it, then make talking, blinking and expression variants. Animated GIFs too.

Stream scene & virtual camera PRO

Layer your avatar, windows and camera, and show them as a Discord camera or screen share.

FAQ

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

Micle is still a pre-release, so until Windows recognizes it as a commonly downloaded app, this blue screen may appear. Click “More info” → “Run anyway” to open it. Only download Micle from the official page (GitHub).

How do I use it with Discord?

Install the free virtual microphone "VB-CABLE" (donationware by VB-Audio Software, distributed separately) and set Discord's input device to "CABLE Output". Micle's first-run guide walks you through it. See How to use a voice changer on Discord.

Does the free version expire or show ads?

No. The free version stays free forever with no ads. You can try Pro features for your first 3 days, and hide features you don't use.

Will it slow down my games?

Turning a feature off also stops its processing. There is a light mode too. AI data is only downloaded when you use those features.

Do I need to install it?

No. Just open the downloaded Micle.exe. If you want it in the Start menu, you can install it from inside the app (no admin rights needed).

Is my voice sent anywhere?

No. Voice processing, transcription and AI all run on your PC. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I activate Pro?

Paste the license key from your purchase email into Settings → About → License key and press Unlock. Only the first check needs the internet.
