About the creator

Hi, I'm fuyohaze (ふよはぜ), the creator of Micle.

How it started

While talking with friends during games, I kept wanting things: a sound effect right now, a funny voice, moving a window behind my game without closing it, opening my Minecraft server quickly.

I built them one by one into a single app, and it became surprisingly handy. So I am sharing it.

What I care about

  • Free forever, no ads: the free version never expires and never shows ads.
  • Runs on your PC: voice, transcription and AI are processed on your PC and never sent out.
  • Turn off what you don't use: with so many features, anything you don't need can be switched off to keep things light.

What comes next

Your requests and feedback are my best guide. Send them anytime from "Feedback" in the app — in any language.
